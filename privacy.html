Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026 · Última actualización: 3 de octubre de 2026

This policy explains how ARISE Miami Seventh-day Adventist Church ("ARISE Miami", "we") handles information in The Journey app and its text messages.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and mobile phone number.
  • Details you give at Level 1 (birthday, gender, marital status, address, how you found ARISE).
  • Your answers in each level: feedback, next steps such as baptism, Life Group help, spiritual gifts, the ministries you choose and the covenant.
  • Whether you agreed to receive text messages, and when.

How we use it

  • To run The Journey: record attendance, keep your progress and send you reminders.
  • So ARISE Miami leaders can follow up on the next steps you ask for.

Where it is kept

Your information is stored in ARISE Miami's church database, Planning Center. Your progress is also saved on your own phone. We use service providers only to run the app and send messages (for example, Twilio for text messages). They may use your information only to provide that service.

Text messages (SMS)

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or text-messaging consent with anyone for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories in this policy exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Text messages are optional. Reply STOP at any time to stop them, or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time.

Questions? Contact ARISE Miami Seventh-day Adventist Church through arisemiami.org.
