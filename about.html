About

The Journey is a four-level discipleship track created by ARISE Miami SDA Church in Miami, Florida: Discover Your Purpose, Discover the Method, Discover Your Giftedness and Discover Your Impact.

El Camino es un recorrido de discipulado en cuatro niveles creado por la Iglesia Adventista ARISE Miami en Miami, Florida.

For churches using a copy

ARISE Miami shares The Journey freely with other churches. You're welcome to adapt the lessons for your congregation. We ask that you keep this page and the credit line in the app.

ARISE Miami comparte El Camino libremente con otras iglesias. Puedes adaptar las lecciones para tu congregación. Te pedimos que conserves esta página y la línea de crédito en la app.

Scripture: Berean Standard Bible (English) and Reina-Valera 1909 (Spanish), both public domain.
