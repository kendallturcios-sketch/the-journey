SMS Opt-In

How people opt in to text messages

People join The Journey in the ARISE Miami app at kendallturcios-sketch.github.io/the-journey/en/participant/ (Spanish: /es/participant/). No login is needed.

  1. On the app's home screen, tap Start here · Sign up for The Journey.
  2. The welcome screen asks for first name, last name, email and mobile phone.
  3. Above the Continue button is an optional, unchecked text-message checkbox. Texts are sent only to people who check it. Leaving it unchecked does not stop anyone from taking part.
